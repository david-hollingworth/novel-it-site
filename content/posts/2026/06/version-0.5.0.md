Version v0.5.0 marks the final feature release before version v1.0.0 and includes [FEAT-07 World-Building](https://david-hollingworth.github.io/novel-it-docs/features/07-world-building/) . This is the final planning module and allows the author to design and document the overall environment in which the story takes place. Relationships can be created between the world-building elements, as well as other the planning elements; characters, items and locations.
